Carbon Monoxide Narrative PSA — "The Silent Killer"

How do you fight a silent killer? Check out our third PSA produced with the Ontario Municipal Fire Prevention Officers Association (OMFPOA) regarding the new changes to carbon monoxide detectors.

Carbon monoxide is odourless, colourless, and deadly — which is exactly what makes it so dangerous. This film takes a dramatic narrative approach to remind Ontario homeowners why updated CO alarm requirements exist, and why compliance could save a life.

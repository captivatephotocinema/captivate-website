Private Markets Forum — Institutional Connect

Last April, Captivate had the pleasure of capturing Institutional Connect's 5th Annual Private Markets Forum in Toronto, Ontario. The event featured 60 speakers and focused on private markets investing in an environment landscape that has been reshaped by geopolitical, climate, and technological forces.

In keynote fireside chats, Nicole Musicco, former Chief Investment Officer from CalPERS, and Pedro Guazo, representative of the secretary-general for the investment of the assets at the United Nations Joint Staff Pension Fund (UNJSPF), each used their unique background and approach to inspire critical thinking when it comes to portfolios and investing.

With a combination of photo and video, Captivate had this three-day dynamic event covered from start to finish.
